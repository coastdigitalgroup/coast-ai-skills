Simulation Guide

For easy demonstration, the inactivity threshold in this sandbox has been reduced to 15 seconds, with a warning countdown appearing during the final 10 seconds.

  1. Sitting Idle: Stop clicking or moving. After 5 seconds, the accessible warning modal will automatically popup.
  2. Keyboard Focus Trap: Try pressing Tab inside the modal. You cannot escape focus to background content.
  3. Multi-Tab Syncing: Open this exact URL in another browser tab. Trigger an interaction in Tab A, and watch the idle counter instantly reset in Tab B!
  4. Page Visibility Resilience: Switch tabs for 15 seconds, then return. The utility will bypass the modal and instantly logout.
