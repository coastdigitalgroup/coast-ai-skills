How to Test the Accessible Version:

  1. Keyboard Test: Tap the Tab key. Notice the focus rings highlight our custom Play button. Pressing Space plays/pauses the audio.
  2. Seek Test: Tab into the timeline slider. Use your ArrowRight or ArrowLeft keys to scrub forward and backward. Note that the custom slider thumbs move correctly and values update.
  3. External Media Interaction Sync: Because we bind directly to the native audio element's play/pause events instead of click events alone, if you play/pause from your OS native controls, the visual button updates perfectly.
