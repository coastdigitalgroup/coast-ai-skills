# B2B Trust Center Audit Checklist

Use this audit checklist to identify friction points, security-communication gaps, and automation leaks in your current B2B compliance presence. Optimizing these areas directly speeds up B2B sales cycles and boosts enterprise lead conversions.

## Part 2: Step-by-Step Audit Questions

### 1. Compliance Visuals & Discovery
- [ ] **Global Footer Link:** Is there a direct, plain-English link in the website's global footer labeled "Security," "Trust," or "Trust Center"?
- [ ] **Logo Scanning:** Do you display recognized visual badges (e.g., SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, FedRAMP) rather than just listing them in a block of text?
- [ ] **Badge Fidelity:** Are the badges up-to-date and clickable to verified registry portals (where applicable, e.g., SOC 2 audit assurance letters)?
- [ ] **Executive Support:** Is there a brief, signed security message from your Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) or CTO to build personal accountability?

### 2. Document Access Flow (The "NDA Gate")
- [ ] **No "Contact Sales" Form Walls:** Can a prospect request a SOC 2 Type II report without being forced to wait for a sales callback?
- [ ] **Automated click-wrap Agreement:** Is the mutual/unilateral NDA presented in-app or in-browser as a "Scroll-and-Accept" click-wrap, rather than a downloadable PDF that requires hand-signatures?
- [ ] **Email Integrity Guard:** Does your document request form automatically block and reject public domains (Gmail, Yahoo, iCloud) to prevent competitors from harvesting sensitive documents?
- [ ] **Instant Secure Delivery:** Upon NDA acceptance, does the file instantly load in a secure browser-viewer or trigger an automated email with a secure, expiring download link?
- [ ] **CRM Syncing:** Does a document download instantly tag the contact as "Enterprise Pipeline - Security Review Initiated" in your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce)?

### 3. Information Architecture & Content Transparency
- [ ] **Data Residency & Geography:** Do you explicitly list where customer data is hosted (e.g., "AWS US-East-1 region") and how data segregation is handled?
- [ ] **Data Security Technical Details:** Is there a section detailing encryption standards (e.g., AES-256 for data at rest, TLS 1.3 for data in transit)?
- [ ] **Operational Uptime Status:** Do you display a real-time status component with historical SLA compliance averages (99.9%+) directly on the Trust page?
- [ ] **Subprocessor List:** Is there a fully public, up-to-date table of third-party subprocessors (e.g., Stripe, Twilio, HubSpot) with descriptions of their service and physical data locations?
- [ ] **Vulnerability Management:** Do you disclose your penetration testing frequency (e.g., "Annual external pen-tests conducted by [Third Party]") and patching cycles?

### 4. Strategic Funnel Hooks
- [ ] **Pricing Page Reassurance:** Does your "Enterprise" tier package list security compliance (SOC 2, ISO) and point directly to your Trust Center?
- [ ] **Demo Form Reinforcement:** Does your Demo Request landing page show security trust badges underneath or beside the submit form?
- [ ] **Sales Enablement Footers:** Do all outbound sales email signatures contain a link to the Trust Center (e.g., "Review our SOC 2 compliance at our [Security Trust Center]")?
- [ ] **Post-Demo Onboarding:** When a prospect finishes their first sales demo, is the automatic follow-up email equipped with a quick link to self-serve the security packet?

### 5. Security FAQ & Questionnaire Deflection
- [ ] **Structured Database:** Is there a searchable list of the top 30-40 security questions your sales team gets asked?
- [ ] **Standard Framework Mapping:** Are these questions categorized using standard security frameworks (e.g., SIG Core, CAIQ, VSA, Tx-RAMP)?
- [ ] **Copy-Paste Friendly:** Are the answers formatted cleanly so that enterprise IT reviewers can copy and paste answers straight into their internal spreadsheets if needed?
