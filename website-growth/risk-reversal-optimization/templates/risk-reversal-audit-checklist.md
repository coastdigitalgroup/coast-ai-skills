# Risk Reversal Audit Checklist

Use this actionable checklist to identify customer commitment barriers, audit existing on-site policies, and strategically place risk-reversal micro-copy on high-value conversion pages.

---

## 1. Risk Spotting & Friction Mapping
Anxiety rises whenever a user is asked to commit money, time, or trust. Audit the page for the following potential sources of friction:

- [ ] **Financial Risk:** Is there high upfront cost or a subscription commitment with no clear escape hatch?
- [ ] **Functional Risk:** Does the buyer have unanswered questions about whether the product/service will actually work for their specific configuration or needs?
- [ ] **Temporal Risk:** Is there a risk that shipping takes too long, setup is too hard, or cancellation takes too many phone calls?
- [ ] **Social/Professional Risk:** If this purchase fails, does the user risk looking incompetent to their boss, team, or family?

---

## 2. On-Site Policy Audit (The "Anti-Legalese" Check)
Review the actual refund, trial, and return copy for tone, transparency, and clarity.

- [ ] **Scannability:** Is the guarantee explained in simple, human-readable copy, or is it buried in dense legal jargon?
- [ ] **Financial Fairness:** Does the return policy require high restocking fees or make the customer pay for return shipping? (If yes, are these fees clearly called out upfront?)
- [ ] **Accessibility of Terms:** Can the refund terms be understood by a layperson in under 10 seconds?
- [ ] **No "Gotchas":** Are there hidden conditions (e.g., "only applies if unopened/unused") that invalidate the primary promise of the guarantee?
- [ ] **Cancellation Flow:** If there is a free trial, is the cancellation process explicitly described as frictionless (e.g., "Cancel in 2 clicks")?

---

## 3. Strategic Placement & Proximity Rules
A great risk-reversal policy does nothing if it remains hidden on a Terms of Service page. Audit where trust signals are displayed:

- [ ] **CTA Proximity:** Is there a clear, reassuring risk-reversal micro-copy block within 50 pixels of the main action button?
- [ ] **Visual Hierarchy:** Does the risk-reversal element support the main value proposition without distracting from the primary CTA button?
- [ ] **Form & Checkout Trust:** Are security badges, privacy guarantees, or money-back badges visible at the exact moment the credit card or personal info is requested?
- [ ] **Cart Drawer Visibility:** For e-commerce, is the return guarantee/shipping policy visible directly inside the cart drawer before the checkout flow begins?

---

## 4. Operational Alignment & Safeguards
Ensure the risk reversal is sustainable and aligned with business logic.

- [ ] **Support Preparedness:** Are support agents trained and empowered to issue refunds or execute returns in accordance with the public guarantee?
- [ ] **Anti-Abuse Safeguards:** Are there logical guardrails (e.g., "Limit one refund per customer household" or "Conditional requirements") to prevent systematic abuse of the policy?
- [ ] **Return Logistics:** Is there a clear, low-friction plan for how physical returns will be picked up, shipped, and restocked or recycled?

---

## Audit Scoring & Action Plan

| Total Score | Level | Action Plan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0 - 5** | **High Friction (Critical)** | The site is suffering from extreme commitment anxiety. Users feel highly vulnerable making a purchase. Immediately introduce a clear 30-day trial or money-back guarantee and place a reassuring micro-copy bullet right under the checkout button. |
| **6 - 11** | **Moderate Friction (Needs Work)** | Trust signals are present but probably buried in the footer or wrapped in complex legalistic language. Translate your policies into friendly, conversational copy and move them next to your primary call-to-actions. |
| **12 - 15** | **Low Friction (Elite)** | Strong risk-reversal. The path to purchase feels exceptionally safe. Run A/B tests to optimize the specific wording of the guarantees (e.g., "100% Money-Back Guarantee" vs. "30-Day Risk-Free Trial") to maximize conversions. |
