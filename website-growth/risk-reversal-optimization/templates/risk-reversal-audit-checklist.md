# Risk Reversal Audit Checklist & Strategy Matrix

This reusable template is designed to help growth marketers, CRO specialists, and product managers audit their current risk profile, evaluate their existing guarantees, and craft high-converting, friction-free risk reversal strategies.

---

## Part 1: Funnel Anxiety & Risk Audit Checklist

Use this checklist to identify where user anxiety is hurting conversions and where risk reversal messaging can be deployed.

### 1. Product Detail Page (PDP) & Pricing Page (The Commitment Phase)
- [ ] **Proximity of Guarantee:** Is there a risk-reversal badge or copy (e.g., "30-Day Money-Back Guarantee") within 50px of the primary Call to Action (CTA)?
- [ ] **Clarity of Terms:** Is the guarantee phrased in plain English (e.g., "Love it or your money back") rather than dense legalese?
- [ ] **No Hidden Costs:** If returns require restocking fees or return shipping charges, are these clearly disclosed before checkout?
- [ ] **Risk-Free Trial Option:** If applicable, can users access a trial without immediately entering a credit card?

### 2. Cart & Checkout (The Payment Phase)
- [ ] **Safety Reinforcement:** Is the checkout button accompanied by reassurance copy (e.g., "Cancel anytime. Safe & secure payment.")?
- [ ] **Policy Transparency:** Is a direct, non-disruptive link to the return/guarantee policy accessible from the checkout footer?
- [ ] **Simple Terms Recap:** Does the order summary explicitly confirm the trial or guarantee period (e.g., "Includes 30-Day Guarantee")?

### 3. Customer Retention & Account Dashboard (The Post-Purchase Phase)
- [ ] **Self-Serve Cancellation:** Can subscribers cancel their trials or recurring plans with a single click inside their dashboard (no phone calls/emails required)?
- [ ] **Easy Refund Requests:** Is the process for requesting a refund clearly documented in the user portal?
- [ ] **Prepaid Label Delivery:** For physical products, does the refund workflow automatically generate a prepaid return label?

## Part 3: Guarantee Selection & Reframing Worksheet

Use this worksheet to reframe weak, defensive policies into strong, conversion-boosting guarantees.

### 1. Choose Your Optimization Type:
- [ ] **Duration Extension:** Shift from 7 or 14 days to a 30, 60, or 90-day period (harnessing the Endowment Effect).
- [ ] **Friction Removal:** Eliminate return shipping fees, restocking fees, or "unopened packaging" requirements.
- [ ] **Aspiration Alignment:** Shift from a standard refund to a performance guarantee (e.g., "Get [Result] or your money back").

### 2. Copy Reframing Worksheet:

| Old / Defensive Policy | New / Benefit-Driven Reframing |

## Part 4: A/B Testing Pitch Card

Use this format to present and get approval for your risk-reversal optimization test.

### **Hypothesis:**
> "By reframing our current defensive **[Old Policy Name]** into a highly prominent and benefit-driven **[New Guarantee Name]**, and placing it in direct visual proximity to the **[Primary CTA Button]**, we will decrease user commitment anxiety, resulting in a **[Target]% lift in Conversion Rate (CVR)** with a negligible increase in actual refund claims."

### **Test Parameters:**
* **Variant A (Control):** Current on-site policies and messaging.
* **Variant B (Challenger):** Reframed benefit-driven guarantee displayed prominently on high-intent pages.
* **Primary Success Metric:** Conversion Rate (CVR)
* **Secondary Metrics:** Cart/Checkout Abandonment Rate, Average Order Value (AOV), Return/Refund Rate, Net Profit per Visitor.
* **Target Audience:** All new visitors.
* **Estimated Runtime:** 14-21 days (or until 95%+ statistical significance is reached).
